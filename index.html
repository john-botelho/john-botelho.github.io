01 — About

Meaningful work, with people I love working with.

I spent nearly a decade in venture banking at Comerica's Technology & Life Sciences division in Boston, rising from analyst to vice president. I underwrote and closed credit facilities for venture- and sponsor-backed technology companies, and built the investor relationships that brought the deals in.

In 2021 my curiosity pulled me into crypto — first through NBA Top Shot, then deep into the communities forming around digital art, on-chain gaming, and DAO governance. In 2022 I chose immersion over an MBA and left banking to build full-time: co-founding Project Frog, helping shape SharkDAO and SquiggleDAO, and working alongside founders, contributors, and users across dozens of projects.

Before any of that, I was the kid writing articles and making graphics for online Madden sim leagues. I still work that way — part analyst, part storyteller, part community builder.
